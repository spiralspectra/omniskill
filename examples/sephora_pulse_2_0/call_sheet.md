# Production Call Sheet: SEPHORA: London Pulse (Midnight Velvet)

- **Logline**: A high-octane, 9:16 vertical fashion collision through rainy midnight Soho, where two avant-garde models strut from London street grit into a gleaming SEPHORA flagship, culminating in a hero product lock.
- **Domain Archetype**: `luxury_fashion`
- **Storytelling Mode**: `Mode A: Parallel Multi-Scene Cut (Montage)`
- **Creative Regime**: `Regime 2: High-Kinetic & Precision Commercial`
- **Total Runtime**: `32s` (4 scenes)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `9:16`

## 1. Visual Continuity & Lighting Setup
- **Color Palette**: #0A0A10, #FF0055, #00F0FF, #FCEE09
- **Master Location**: Midnight Soho to SEPHORA Flagship
- **Spatial Architecture**: Exterior: Rain-slicked London cobblestones and wet asphalt reflecting neon signage. Interior: High-gloss black lacquer and mirrored vanity aisles bathed in brilliant 5600K beauty lighting.
- **Lighting Setup**: Exterior: Moody 3200K tungsten streetlamps with electric magenta and cyan neon rim light. Interior: 5600K high-CRI diffused beauty lighting.
- **Atmospheric Particles**: Fine airborne London rain drizzle, water droplets on patent leather, cosmetic shimmer powder catching light.

## 2. Character & Subject Roster
- **Model Maya (The Neon Siren)**: Tall, athletic, razor-sharp high cheekbones, graphic slicked-back raven architectural bob. | Wardrobe Polarity: HIGH POLARITY A: Vivid high-gloss scarlet-red patent trench coat with oversized structured lapels, sheer black mesh collar, and metallic silver pointed-toe stiletto boots. | Movement: Aggressive, predatory, fast-tempo runway stride with zero hip sway and sharp head pivots.

## 4. Musical Score & Acoustic Direction
- **Track Title**: Midnight Velvet: UK Garage Pulse (Syncopated UK Garage / Hyper-Pop Runway Sizzle)
- **Tempo & Key**: 138 BPM in F Minor
- **Instrumentation**: Syncopated 2-step drum groove with punchy kick and crisp rimshots, Deep rolling Moog Reese bassline, Glittering hyper-pop synth arpeggios and vocal chops, Dramatic snare roll risers and white-noise sweeps
- **Dynamic Arc**: Seconds 00-08: Snappy 2-step rhythm kicks in on the wink. Seconds 08-16: Heavy bassline drops inside flagship. Seconds 16-24: Massive riser sweep building energy. Seconds 24-32: Sudden cut to dead silence on final freeze-frame bag snap, leaving only a sub-bass decay.
