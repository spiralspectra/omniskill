# Production Call Sheet: IKEA: The Living Room Unfolds

- **Logline**: In an empty sunlit Scandinavian apartment, an iconic flat-pack IKEA box violently trembles, then mechanically unpacks and self-assembles into a pristine, serene living sanctuary with hyper-tactile ASMR foley.
- **Domain Archetype**: `architectural_sanctuary`
- **Storytelling Mode**: `Mode A: Parallel Multi-Scene Cut (Montage)`
- **Creative Regime**: `Regime 2: High-Kinetic & Precision Commercial`
- **Total Runtime**: `32s` (4 scenes)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9`

## 1. Visual Continuity & Lighting Setup
- **Color Palette**: #1E293B, #F59E0B, #0284C7, #F8FAFC
- **Master Location**: Minimalist Stockholm Loft
- **Spatial Architecture**: An expansive 8x6 meter open-plan apartment with ultra-smooth pale blonde ash hardwood floors, matte-white limewashed walls, and floor-to-ceiling multi-pane Nordic windows on the left wall looking out to quiet birch trees.
- **Lighting Setup**: Soft, low-angled morning Scandinavian sunlight streaming through the windows at 5200K daylight balance, casting elongated warm rectangular light pools across the floor, filled with soft 6500K ambient room bounce.
- **Atmospheric Particles**: Delicate, slow-drifting micro-motes of atmospheric dust illuminated in the light beam; zero haze, zero smoke.

## 4. Musical Score & Acoustic Direction
- **Track Title**: Nordic Serenity: Tactile Pulse (Minimalist Acoustic Neo-Classical ASMR)
- **Tempo & Key**: 84 BPM in D Major
- **Instrumentation**: Plucked acoustic cello pizzicato, Felted upright piano playing delicate sparse arpeggios, Subtle wooden xylophone clicks tuned to foley impacts, Warm analog sub-bass drone resolving into absolute stillness
- **Dynamic Arc**: Seconds 00-08: Tense, playful pizzicato rhythm ticking like a clockwork box. Seconds 08-16: Rapid, polyrhythmic wooden mallet pulses synchronizing with furniture clicks. Seconds 16-24: Swelling warm cello and piano harmony. Seconds 24-32: All music decrescendos instantly on the final blanket drop, leaving only a sustained pure piano chord and serene silence.
