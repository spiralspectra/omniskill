# Production Call Sheet: SEPHORA: London Pulse (Continuous 40s Oner)

- **Logline**: A seamless, unbroken 40-second continuous 9:16 vertical Steadicam oner tracking two avant-garde models through rainy midnight Soho into the SEPHORA flagship and back to the wet pavement, linked across four +10s Gemini Omni extensions.
- **Domain Archetype**: `luxury_fashion`
- **Storytelling Mode**: `Mode B: Continuous Scene Extension Chain (Oner)`
- **Creative Regime**: `Regime 2: High-Kinetic & Precision Commercial`
- **Total Runtime**: `40s` (4 scenes)
- **Aspect Ratio**: `9:16`

## 1. Visual Continuity & Lighting Setup
- **Color Palette**: #0A0A10, #FF0055, #00F0FF, #FCEE09
- **Master Location**: Midnight Soho to SEPHORA Flagship
- **Spatial Architecture**: Exterior: Rain-slicked London cobblestones and wet asphalt reflecting neon signage. Interior: High-gloss black lacquer and mirrored vanity aisles bathed in brilliant 5600K beauty lighting.
- **Lighting Setup**: Exterior: Moody 3200K tungsten streetlamps with electric magenta and cyan neon rim light. Interior: 5600K high-CRI diffused beauty lighting.
- **Atmospheric Particles**: Fine airborne London rain drizzle, water droplets on patent leather, cosmetic shimmer powder catching light.

## 4. Musical Score & Acoustic Direction
- **Track Title**: Midnight Velvet: UK Garage Pulse (40s Continuous Oner) (Syncopated UK Garage / Hyper-Pop Runway Sizzle)
- **Tempo & Key**: 138 BPM in F Minor
- **Instrumentation**: Syncopated 2-step drum groove with punchy kick and crisp rimshots, Deep rolling Moog Reese bassline, Glittering hyper-pop synth arpeggios and vocal chops, Dramatic snare roll risers and white-noise sweeps
- **Dynamic Arc**: Seconds 00-10: Intimate street rain ambiance and skipping 2-step groove during Soho approach. Seconds 10-20: Rolling Reese bass drops as models enter the flagship, whispering 'London, wake up'. Seconds 20-30: Soaring hyper-pop synth arpeggios and white-noise riser as bag is lifted. Seconds 30-40: Climactic percussion impact at second 38 snapping into dead silence on bag freeze.
